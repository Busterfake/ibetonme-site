What the app is, and isn't

I Bet On Me is a self-help tool to support you in stopping gambling. It is not a medical treatment, therapy or a crisis service, and it doesn't replace a professional. If you feel out of control, or think about harming yourself, contact a helpline or emergency services. The app shows a free helpline for your country.

Who can use it

You must be an adult (at least 18, or the age of majority where you live) to use the app.

Figures in the app

"Money kept" and the examples next to it are estimates based on what you said you used to bet. They are meant to motivate you, not to be exact financial figures.
